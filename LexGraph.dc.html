What LexGraph is — and is not

We are honest about the line, because the difference matters when you are deciding what to do next.

It is legal information

Explanations of what a situation usually means, which law applies, and what people normally do next.

It is not a lawyer

No advocate reviews your situation here, nothing you write is filed anywhere, and an AI answer can be incomplete or wrong. For anything with a deadline or a court date, talk to a qualified advocate.
